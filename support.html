Frequently Asked Questions

Does BLELITE require an account?

No. BLELITE never requires an account, registration, or login. You install it and start using it. Your data stays on your device.

Is my portfolio data sent to a server?

No. All your positions, journal entries, photos, and preferences are stored locally on your iPhone. Nothing is transmitted to any server.

Does BLELITE include ads or trackers?

No. The app contains no advertising trackers, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no ad networks. It's a focused, private experience.

Which markets does BLELITE cover?

US, Hong Kong, Japan, and European equities, with a multi-currency FX converter covering 10 major currencies.

How do I back up or move my data?

BLELITE data is included in your standard iOS device backup when iCloud or iTunes backup is enabled. To fully remove data, simply uninstall the app.

What iOS version is required?

BLELITE is tuned for iOS 16 and later on iPhone. It uses the latest design patterns and system features for a polished experience.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email us at selmabarankolay@gmail.com with a description and screenshots if possible. We review every report.

Can I delete all my data?

Yes. Uninstalling BLELITE permanently removes all locally stored data from your device.
