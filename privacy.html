Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Our Promise

BLELITE never requires an account, never transmits your positions to a server, and contains no advertising trackers. Your investment data stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

BLELITE is designed with a privacy-first architecture. The app collects the following categories of information:

  • On-Device Data Only: All your portfolio positions, trade journal entries, photos, screenshots, calculations, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device using secure local storage.
  • No Personal Identification: We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any personally identifiable information.
  • No Account Required: BLELITE does not require registration, login, or any form of account creation.

2. How We Use Your Information

Since all sensitive data remains on your device, we do not access, view, transmit, or process your portfolio positions, trade history, photos, or any personal financial information. Your data is used only by you, on your device, to power the app's features.

3. Photo and Camera Access

If you choose to use the photo trade journal feature, BLELITE will request permission to access your camera and/or photo library. Photos you capture or select are:

  • Stored locally on your device only
  • Never uploaded to any server or cloud service
  • Never shared with third parties
  • Only accessible to you within the BLELITE app

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings without affecting app functionality.

4. Analytics and Tracking

BLELITE does not include:

  • Advertising trackers
  • Third-party analytics SDKs that profile users
  • Behavioral tracking pixels
  • Cross-app or cross-site tracking mechanisms
  • Ad networks of any kind

5. Third-Party Services

BLELITE uses minimal third-party services only for essential functionality. Market price data shown in the app is simulated or sourced from public reference data; no user-identifying information is sent to any third party.

6. Data Security

Your data is protected by:

  • iOS sandboxing — your data is isolated from other apps
  • Local encryption through iOS file protection
  • No cloud transmission — eliminating server-side breach risk
  • Optional device passcode/biometric lock protection

7. Children's Privacy

BLELITE is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. The app contains no content targeting minors.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the app at any time
  • Delete: Uninstalling the app permanently removes all locally stored data
  • Export: Your portfolio and journal data remains under your control
  • Permissions: You may revoke camera and photo access at any time through iOS Settings

9. International Users

Because BLELITE does not transmit your personal or financial data to any server, there are no cross-border data transfers to govern. Your data resides entirely on your device, in your jurisdiction.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Because we collect no contact information, we cannot notify users directly; please review this page periodically.
